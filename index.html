🏏 Australian Sports Culture

Who plays? Who watches? Is the money well spent?
Exploring participation, attendance trends, and funding efficiency in Australian sports

13 Interactive Visualisations 1 Geographic Map 3 Custom Charts

📖 How to Read This Visualisation

What

We visualise eight datasets from AusPlay, ABS, and ASC covering Australian sports participation, attendance, funding, and venue usage (2019–2025).

Why

To reveal participation gaps by age, gender, and state; analyse the return on public funding; and suggest pathways to grow sports engagement.

How

Colour encodes sport type; geography reveals state culture; interactivity lets you explore details via hover tooltips and click-to-filter legends.

📋 Key Findings & Recommendations

Youth Participation is Critical

Peak participation occurs at ages 5–14. Invest in school sports programs to maintain lifelong engagement.

Significant Gender Gaps

AFL and Soccer show 10–15 percentage point male skew. Increase female participation through targeted programs.

Optimise Funding Efficiency

Basketball and Tennis show lower ROI. Review funding allocation or set performance benchmarks.
